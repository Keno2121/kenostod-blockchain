name: IaaS Partnership Strategy
description: How Kenostod's partnerships with chains and exchanges should be framed — infrastructure first, token listing second
---

## Core Rule
Lead every exchange/chain partnership with IaaS, not token listing. Listing conversations put Kenostod in a subordinate position (asking for access). IaaS conversations position Kenostod as an infrastructure provider (offering value).

## Products to Lead With
- **SOE** — hybrid AMM + Order Book DEX, deployable on any chain as native trading infrastructure
- **UTL Protocol** — fee transparency and redistribution layer, sits on top of any chain's transaction layer
- **FAL / FALP** — flash loan infrastructure for DeFi protocols building on partner chains
- **VLAT** — market intelligence scoring layer (Volatility, Liquidity, Arbitrage, Timing)

## What NOT to Lead With
- Solar Bunker — not fully set up yet; reserved for Africa market
- KENO token listing — comes after infrastructure partnership is established
- Liquidity discussions — these are secondary to product integration conversations

## Applied to Active Partners
- **BOT Chain** — pitch SOE as their native DEX infrastructure; UTL as transaction layer; FAL for DeFi protocols building on BOT Chain
- **LBank** — pitch UTL for fee transparency on their trading pairs; VLAT as market intelligence for their traders

**Why:** Token listing requires paying fees and having volume. IaaS partnerships require having a product. Kenostod has the product. This is the correct leverage point at this stage.
