## Strategy

Treat ParaSwap and 1inch as complementary channels:

- ParaSwap already powers the UTL dashboard's BSC swap flow.
- Pursue an official 1inch integration or partnership rather than attempting to modify 1inch's hosted interface.
- Use opt-in landing pages, partner embeds, referral/source parameters, and post-swap calls to action to introduce UTL staking, FALP, and related products.

FALP should be presented to 1inch as a complementary liquidity and user-activation product unless 1inch formally approves a direct adapter or liquidity-source integration. An aggregator route and a lending/arbitrage pool are not automatically interchangeable.

The preferred 1inch pitch is the "1inch Education Route": demonstrate that FAL detects cross-DEX opportunities outside current routing graphs, propose an approved FALP integration, and connect educated graduates and the Africa curriculum rollout to incremental 1inch activity. This is a proposal, not a claim that the route is live.

**Why:** The user wants to reach both ParaSwap and 1inch users, but neither aggregator's hosted website can be edited unilaterally. The growth path must preserve the existing ParaSwap channel while adding an approved 1inch relationship.

**How to apply:** In partner proposals, offer mutual value: Kenostod brings KENO liquidity, education, and qualified ecosystem users; the aggregator provides execution and distribution. Never imply access to user identities or promise routed volume before approval.
