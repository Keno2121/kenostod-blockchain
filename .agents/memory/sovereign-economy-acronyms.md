## Canonical terms

- **UTL** = Universal Transaction Layer
- **SOE** = Sovereign Order Engine
- **VLAT** = Volume Liquidity Adoption Time

## Rule

Use these expansions in external proposals, partner outreach, product briefs, and new marketing material. Treat alternate expansions in older project pages as legacy wording until those pages are reconciled.

**Why:** The user explicitly corrected the terminology after discovering that older IaaS materials used inconsistent names. Accurate naming is important for the Biswap partnership brief and future partner conversations.

**How to apply:** Describe UTL as the transaction-layer infrastructure, SOE as the order-engine/orchestration product, and VLAT as the volume-liquidity-adoption-time measurement framework. Do not silently reuse the older “Universal Token Lifecycle,” “Sovereign Operating Environment,” or “Value Lifecycle Automation Toolset” expansions.
