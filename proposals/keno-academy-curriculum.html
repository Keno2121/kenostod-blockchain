Capstone
Completion Verification

The platform knows — no supervisor required

Every KENO reward is released by a smart contract, not by a teacher or administrator. Completion is verified automatically — it's tamper-proof, instant, and leaves a permanent record on the blockchain.

📝

Step 1 — Complete the Module & Take the Quiz

Each module ends with a scored assessment. Students must pass a minimum threshold (set per tier — e.g., 80% for Bronze) before the next step can trigger. The quiz is timed, randomised, and cannot be shared in advance.

Assessment Gate
⚙️

Step 2 — Smart Contract Validates the Result

When a passing score is recorded, the academy system calls the KENO reward contract on-chain. The contract checks: (1) has this student wallet already claimed this tier? (2) is the score above threshold? Only if both conditions are true does it proceed.

On-Chain Logic · Cannot Be Overridden
🪙

Step 3 — KENO Appears in the Student's Wallet

The contract sends the tier reward directly to the student's wallet address — no middleman, no approval queue. The student can see the transaction on-chain within seconds. Each wallet can only receive each tier's reward once.

Instant · Non-Custodial
📋

Step 4 — Permanent Credential Record

The blockchain transaction is the credential. It carries the student's wallet address, the tier completed, the timestamp, and the reward amount. This record is public, permanent, and can be verified by any employer or institution without contacting the school.

Lifelong Record · Verifiable by Anyone
No supervisor needed. The contract enforces the rules. A teacher cannot manually grant a reward, and a student cannot receive a tier twice. The system is designed so that gaming it is mathematically harder than simply completing the course.
Student Accounts

Your wallet is your account — no password, ever

There is no username or password to forget. A student's blockchain wallet is their identity on the platform. Everything — progress, earnings, credentials — is tied to that wallet address and accessible from any device, anywhere in the world.

🔑

First time: Set up a wallet

During onboarding, students install a free browser wallet (MetaMask or similar) and create a wallet address. This takes about 10 minutes with the guided setup video. The wallet generates a 12-word recovery phrase — that is the only "password" needed, ever.

📱

Every time after: One click to connect

On any subsequent visit — from school, home, or mobile — the student clicks "Connect Wallet," approves the connection in their wallet app, and they are instantly logged in. Their full progress and earnings are already there.

📊

Progress is on-chain

Completed tiers, earned KENO, and staking positions are recorded on the blockchain — not in a database that can be lost or reset. A student who changes schools, transfers to university, or returns years later still has their full record.

🏫

Teachers get a class dashboard

Educators connect their own admin wallet to view all enrolled student addresses, which tiers they have completed, and aggregate class progress — without ever seeing or controlling student funds. View-only, by design.
