Track 03 — Banking Infrastructure

Funding a Digital Bank

Two paths to banking: get a charter (you become the bank) or use a BaaS partner (you plug into someone else's banking license). The right path depends on your timeline and capital. Here is the full breakdown.

Path A — Banking as a Service (BaaS) — Start Here
Launch in Months, Not Years
BaaS means you plug your B.U.K. card into an existing bank's infrastructure. You get all the features of a bank account without needing a charter. This is how Chime, Current, and thousands of other fintechs operate. Use this to launch and prove the model while the charter application processes.
ALREADY IN ECOSYSTEM
Cybrid
Canada — Toronto, ON (US operations)
Cybrid is already referenced in the B.U.K. documents as the BaaS layer for the KUTL Card. Using the same BaaS provider for both B.U.K. and KUTL Card means one unified account dashboard — exactly what the blueprint describes. Cybrid provides crypto-enabled banking, which means KENO token integration is native, not bolted on.
Why This is the First Call
You already have a relationship pathway here through KUTL Card. Expand the existing conversation: "We want to extend the Cybrid integration to include a second card program — the B.U.K. Security Banking card — with the same underlying account structure." cybrid.app → Contact Sales.

Their minimum is lower than legacy BaaS providers and they have a formal startup program. CPI Card Group (Track 1 target) already integrates with Unit — meaning card manufacturing and banking could be bundled.

Path B — Wyoming SPDI Charter — The Long Game
Become the Bank — Full Control, Full Revenue
The Wyoming SPDI (Special Purpose Depository Institution) is already in your roadmap. It takes 18–24 months and $5–10M minimum capital but gives you a full banking license. This is the endgame — B.U.K. becomes its own chartered bank and keeps 100% of interchange and deposits.

🏛️ Wyoming SPDI — Why Wyoming Specifically
→
Crypto-native by law: Wyoming passed the SPDI Act specifically to allow crypto-friendly banks. A Wyoming SPDI can legally hold both USD deposits and digital assets — meaning B.U.K. can hold KENO balances alongside USD without needing separate crypto licensing.
→
No insurance requirement: SPDI institutions do not require FDIC insurance, which eliminates one of the most expensive requirements for new banks. Instead, they operate as custodial institutions with 100% reserve backing.
→
Precedents already set: Kraken Bank and Custodia Bank both received Wyoming SPDI charters. The regulatory pathway is proven — it is not experimental.
