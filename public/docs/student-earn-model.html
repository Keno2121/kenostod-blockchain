Kenostod Academy — Partner Brief

How Students Earn, Stake, and Grow on the Academy Platform

An interim earning model designed to protect students from token volatility while KENO liquidity matures.

Confidential — For Partner Use

Kenostod Academy rewards students with KENO tokens for completing verified courses. Rather than directing students to immediately sell KENO on thin markets, we have designed a stake-to-earn model that converts course rewards into a passive income stream in BOT — the native currency of BOT Chain — which is a separate, independently valued asset. This approach protects students, builds long-term engagement, and remains viable even during early-stage token liquidity.

The Student Journey — Step by Step
1

Enrol & Complete a Course

Students join through a school partnership link and complete courses at their own pace. Each verified completion is recorded on the platform.

2

Earn 250 KENO Per Course

Upon completion, the student's connected wallet receives 250 KENO automatically. KENO is the platform's native reward token, live on BOT Chain.

250 KENO per course · No expiry · No fees
3

Stake KENO in the Flash Arbitrage Loan Pool (FALPool)

Instead of selling immediately, students deposit their KENO into the FALPool smart contract on BOT Chain. This takes less than 30 seconds. Four lock options are available — flexible or fixed terms of 7, 30, or 90 days — with longer locks earning a higher reward multiplier (up to 1.618×, the Golden Ratio tier).

4

Earn BOT Rewards Automatically

The platform's arbitrage bot trades on the BOT Chain exchange (BDEX) and deposits a portion of every profit directly into FALPool. Stakers receive their proportional share of these profits in BOT — the chain's native token — continuously, every time the bot earns.

BOT rewards accumulate silently — no action required
5

Claim BOT or Withdraw KENO When Ready

Students can claim their accumulated BOT rewards at any time, or wait until their lock period ends to also withdraw their original KENO. There is no penalty for flexible deposits. BOT can be held, used on the BOT Chain network, or converted through available exchanges.

🛡 What protects the student?

BOT rewards are generated from real trading activity, not token inflation. Even if KENO's market price fluctuates, the BOT reward stream continues independently as long as the arb bot is active.

📱 What does it look like?

Students connect a wallet (MetaMask or WalletConnect), complete a course, and tap "Stake KENO" on their earnings page. BOT rewards appear automatically. No trading knowledge required.

Why We Are Doing It This Way

KENO is currently in its early liquidity phase. The trading pools that support KENO are live but intentionally thin — meaning a large number of students selling KENO simultaneously could move the price significantly. Rather than expose students to that risk, we designed the FALPool model so that students benefit from the protocol's revenue without needing to sell. As trading volume on BOT Chain grows, KENO liquidity deepens naturally — at which point students will also have a straightforward KENO → BOT → fiat path with minimal slippage. This transition will be communicated clearly to all partners before it is opened.
