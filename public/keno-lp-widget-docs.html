Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about providing KENO liquidity.

What is impermanent loss?
Impermanent loss occurs when the price ratio between your two assets changes after you deposit. If KENO rises significantly relative to BNB (or vice versa), you may receive less of the gaining asset when you withdraw than if you had just held. This risk is standard across all AMM pools. LP fees and KENO burn appreciation can offset impermanent loss over time.
Does the widget hold my funds?
No. The widget calls PancakeSwap's official Router contract (0x10ED…) directly via MetaMask. Your funds go on-chain through PancakeSwap's own contracts — Kenostod never touches them. You can verify every transaction on BscScan.
How does the 15% AutoBurn split work?
KENOAutoBurn (0x9Fb4…) is a separate on-chain contract that periodically sweeps accumulated LP fees and burns 15% worth of KENO. This happens automatically — you don't need to do anything. The burn projection in the widget is an estimate based on current pool volume.
How do I remove my liquidity?
Open PancakeSwap V2 → Liquidity → find KENO/BNB → click Remove. Your LP tokens are in your wallet and can be redeemed any time at your proportional share of the pool's current reserves.
What is the KENO token contract address?
KENO on BSC: 0x48bb049afe50b050b458624dc6233acd51024ab4
Can I embed this on my own site?
Yes — one script tag. The widget is self-contained and works on any HTML page. It renders a floating 🔥 button, which opens the full LP panel with live on-chain data. See the Embed section for the snippet.
What is the KENO Market Maker badge?
After your first successful liquidity add, the widget generates a shareable achievement card showing your pool share %, estimated KENO burned per month, and the "Powered by UTL" mark. Share it on X/Twitter to let the community know you're supporting the pool.
