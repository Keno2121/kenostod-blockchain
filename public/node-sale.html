How Node Yield Works

Simple, transparent, and on-chain verifiable

1

Bots Trade Daily

Flash Orb and Live Arb continuously scan PancakeSwap, BiSwap, and other BSC DEXs for arb opportunities and execute profitable trades.

2

10% Allocated to Nodes

Each day at UTC midnight, 10% of the bot wallet's realised profit is set aside for node distribution. The remaining 90% compounds bot capital.

3

Split Among Holders

The node pool is divided equally among all 20 nodes. Your share = node pool ÷ 20, regardless of when you bought in.

BNB Sent to Your Wallet

Distributions are sent in BNB directly to the wallet holding your node NFT. No staking, no claiming — it arrives automatically.

Common Questions

Why only 20 nodes?

20 nodes keeps the yield meaningful per holder. A larger node count dilutes each share to the point where daily distributions become negligible. 20 nodes at 0.5 BNB raises 10 BNB — enough to meaningfully deepen the KENO/BNB liquidity pool and expand bot capital.

Are the bots actually live right now?

Yes. Flash Orb and Live Arb have been running on BSC since before this node sale launched. They scan PancakeSwap, BiSwap, and other BSC DEXs continuously. You can verify bot wallet activity at BscScan: 0xC20b9a51BdedBd21CBE28E68c1089438D21c8cf2

What network does the node NFT live on?

BNB Smart Chain (BSC). You need MetaMask or any BSC-compatible wallet, with 0.5 BNB for the node price plus a small amount of BNB for gas (~0.003 BNB).

Can I sell my node?

Yes. The KNODE NFT is a standard ERC-721 token and can be listed on any BSC NFT marketplace. Yield distributions go to whoever currently holds the NFT at midnight UTC each day.

How are KENO allocations delivered?

Each node includes 10,000 KENO at listing price ($0.05/KENO = $500 value). KENO is now live on PancakeSwap — swap here or view chart. Allocations are sent to your wallet within 24 hours of minting.

What happens when all 20 nodes sell?

The smart contract closes the sale automatically. No new nodes can be minted. The 10 BNB raised goes directly to deepen the KENO/BNB liquidity pool and scale bot capital — which increases yield for all existing node holders.
