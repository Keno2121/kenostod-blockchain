Get Your Phor-Cloft Paperwork in Order
$0 if you have them · $150–$300 attorney if not 1 week Do first — everything depends on this

Before you can put anything into Phor-Cloft Holdings, you need three documents that prove you are the owner and manager. Pull these from Georgia Secretary of State or wherever you formed it.

  • Articles of Organization Filed with Georgia SOS on Feb 3, 2025. Download the stamped copy from sos.ga.gov — you'll need it for everything.
  • Operating Agreement Internal document that names you as the sole member and manager. If you don't have one yet, create it now — it's not filed publicly but banks and attorneys require it.
  • EIN (Employer Identification Number) Your federal tax ID for Phor-Cloft. Free from IRS.gov (takes 10 minutes online). You need this to open a business bank account.
  • Registered Agent (Georgia) Must be a Georgia address. If you're not in GA, services like Northwest Registered Agent (~$125/yr) handle this. Check your SOS filing — it's listed there.
✅ Action: Download your Articles from sos.ga.gov → Search "Phor-Cloft Holdings" → Download the filing. Apply for EIN at irs.gov/businesses/small if you don't have one.

Open a Business Bank Account for Phor-Cloft
$0 — no minimum balance 1–3 days DIY — fully online

Phor-Cloft Holdings needs its own bank account. This becomes the central account — it receives management fees from all subsidiaries, investor deposits, and licensing royalties. All other entity accounts flow upward into this one.

  • Mercury Bank — mercury.com Best for tech/crypto-adjacent companies. No fees, free wires, API access, multi-user. Apply with your EIN + Articles of Organization. Approved in 1–3 business days.
  • Relay Financial — relayfi.com Good alternative if Mercury declines. Multiple sub-accounts, good for separating income streams from different subsidiaries.
✅ Action: Go to mercury.com → Apply for a business account → Select LLC → Upload EIN confirmation + Georgia Articles of Organization. Takes 10 minutes to apply.
⚠️ Note: Georgia is not as crypto-friendly as Wyoming. When asked about business type, describe Phor-Cloft as a "technology holding company and software licensor" — not as a crypto company. It owns equity, not crypto.

Transfer Membership of Kenostod Academy LLC to Phor-Cloft
$0 DIY · $500–$1,500 attorney 1–2 weeks Recommend attorney for this one

Right now, you personally own Kenostod Blockchain Academy LLC (Wyoming). You need to transfer that membership interest to Phor-Cloft Holdings LLC so it sits underneath the holding company. This is a one-page legal document.

  • Assignment of Membership Interest Transfers your personal ownership of Kenostod Academy LLC to Phor-Cloft Holdings LLC. You sign as both the Assignor (you personally) and on behalf of Assignee (you as manager of Phor-Cloft). No state filing required — it's an internal document.
  • Update Kenostod Academy Operating Agreement Amend the Academy's operating agreement to reflect that the sole member is now Phor-Cloft Holdings LLC (Georgia) instead of you personally. You remain the manager/operator.
  • Wyoming Annual Report Update On next Wyoming annual report (due on the anniversary of formation), list Phor-Cloft Holdings LLC as the member. Not urgent — can wait for next renewal.
✅ Action: Search "Assignment of Membership Interest template" on Rocket Lawyer or LegalZoom — about $50 for a template. Or hire a business attorney for ~$500 to draft it cleanly. Sign, date, file in your personal records.
⚠️ Important: After this transfer, you personally own Phor-Cloft. Phor-Cloft owns Academy. You still control everything — just through the holding structure instead of directly.

Assign All Intellectual Property to Phor-Cloft
$200–$800 depending on filings 1–2 weeks DIY with templates

This is one of the most powerful moves. By assigning all IP to Phor-Cloft Holdings, every subsidiary then licenses the IP from Phor-Cloft. This creates a royalty income stream flowing directly to you as the holding company owner.

  • IP Assignment Agreement Assigns the following from you personally (or from Academy LLC) to Phor-Cloft Holdings: KENO brand + logo, E-Fi™ term, FAL™ system, PoRV™ protocol, B.U.K. dual-chip concept, all source code copyrights, domain names (kenostodblockchain.com, etc.).
  • Trademark Applications (USPTO) File: "E-Fi" (Class 36 — financial services, Class 41 — education), "FAL" (Class 36), "B.U.K." (Class 36). Each filing: $250–$350. Assign applicant as Phor-Cloft Holdings LLC. Total: ~$750–$1,050 in filing fees.
  • Domain & Account Transfers Transfer ownership of kenostodblockchain.com (and any others) at your domain registrar to Phor-Cloft Holdings. Update billing contacts for Replit, Render, GitHub to reflect Phor-Cloft as the entity.
  • Patent Provisional Applications File provisional patents for FAL™ system and B.U.K. dual-chip card. Each provisional: $320 (micro-entity fee). Gives 12 months of "Patent Pending" status. Applicant = Phor-Cloft Holdings LLC.
✅ Action: Start with the IP Assignment Agreement — it costs nothing and protects everything immediately. Then file trademarks at teas.uspto.gov. Trademark your brand NOW before someone else does.

  • Management Services Agreement (MSA) Phor-Cloft provides management, strategy, and administrative services to each subsidiary for a fixed monthly fee. Legitimate business expense for the subsidiary, income for Phor-Cloft.
  • IP License Agreement Each subsidiary gets a license from Phor-Cloft to use the brand, trademarks, and technology in exchange for a royalty percentage. Standard rate: 3–8% of gross revenue depending on how IP-dependent the business is.

Register T.D.I.R. Foundation (Marshall Islands) & Link to Phor-Cloft
$3,000–$5,000 one-time 4–8 weeks Requires Marshall Islands agent

Once Phor-Cloft is organized (Steps 1–5 done), form the T.D.I.R. Foundation offshore. Then transfer a portion of your Phor-Cloft membership to the Foundation. The Foundation becomes the top-level owner — Phor-Cloft becomes its US subsidiary.

  • Marshall Islands Registered Agent You need a Marshall Islands registered agent to file. Recommended firms: Tango (tangomarshalleslands.com), MIHO (Marshall Islands corporate services). They handle formation for $2,000–$3,500 including government fees.
  • Foundation Charter / Constitution The founding document. Names T.D.I.R. Foundation's purpose, beneficiaries (the ecosystem and its participants), supervisory council (you as Founder + 1–2 others), and the relationship to Phor-Cloft Holdings LLC.
  • Membership Interest Transfer: You → T.D.I.R. Foundation After forming the Foundation, transfer 51–100% of your Phor-Cloft membership to T.D.I.R. Foundation. You retain control as the Foundation's Founder/Director. This makes the entire stack foundation-owned offshore.
  • KENO Token Governance Assignment T.D.I.R. Foundation becomes the official token governance authority. The Foundation's DAO (KENO holders) votes on major protocol decisions. This legitimizes the token under Marshall Islands DAO law.
✅ Action: Do Steps 1–5 first. Then contact a Marshall Islands corporate agent. Tell them: "I want to register a non-profit foundation under Marshall Islands DAO law. It will own a US LLC (Phor-Cloft Holdings, Georgia)." Budget 6–8 weeks.
