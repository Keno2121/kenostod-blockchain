05 · Payment safety

A controlled pause before settlement can prevent an irreversible user mistake.

UTL Reversal Pool

Eligible UTL transfers can enter a pending or escrow-backed state for a disclosed period before final settlement.

  • Standard flow provides a five-minute reversal window
  • Authorized sender can request cancellation before expiry
  • Principal refund and any disclosed fee are shown transparently
  • Status can be queried by transaction hash
Important boundary

Safety without rewriting the chain

This feature does not roll back a finalized blockchain transaction. It operates at the application and settlement layer before finalization, with eligibility, authorization, timing, and fee rules enforced by the deployment.

LIVE · UTL product flow PARTNER · adapter and pilot required

Ideal for: stablecoin payments · remittances · merchant checkout · wallet transfers

VLAT turns volume, liquidity, adoption, and time into a shared operating view. It supports composite scores, ecosystem phases, revenue signals, feeds, snapshots, and alerts.

SOE

Sovereign Order Engine

SOE is the orchestration layer for protocol operations. It coordinates monitoring, permissions, alerts, state, bot workflows, and recurring reports in one operational environment.
