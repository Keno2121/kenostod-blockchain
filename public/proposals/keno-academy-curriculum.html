Student Earning Mechanics

Real income from real protocols

Every tool students learn is live on the blockchain. Their earnings are theirs — not points, not gift cards. Real assets with spending power for rent, food, and school supplies.

🪙

KENO Token Rewards

Students earn KENO at every curriculum milestone. KENO is a live BSC token with real market value — students can hold, trade, or stake it immediately upon earning it.

⚡

FAL Income Education

Flash Arbitrage Loans (FAL) are automated income tools that capture price differences across markets in a single transaction. Students learn to read, simulate, and eventually run FAL strategies — a skill with real earning potential.

🔄

UTL Passive Income

Students stake KENO in UTL and receive a 0.1% fee from every transaction processed through the protocol. As volume grows, their passive income grows — teaching compounding and protocol economics in a live environment.
