The Model

Solar Power meets Web3

Four components that work together to bring financial sovereignty to off-grid Africa.

1
📦
The Container
A ruggedised shipping container houses everything — solar panels on the roof, batteries inside, WiFi antennas on the sides. Drops into any community, no grid connection required.
2
☀️
Solar Generation
Panels generate 4–8 kW depending on tier. Excess power is stored in lithium batteries providing 12–24 hours of autonomous operation. The community gets free electricity and WiFi.
3
📡
Mesh Network
Each Bunker acts as a mesh node. Neighbouring units relay data for each other during outages — earning KENO for every hour of uptime. The network gets stronger the more units deploy.
4
🎓
Earn while Learning
Students access the Kenostod Blockchain Academy offline-first. Completing courses earns KENO. The community owns the node — they stake KENO to activate it and earn resilience rewards continuously.

Hardware Partnership

What the Hardware Partner Provides

We handle the blockchain protocol, KENO rewards, education content, and software. You handle the physical infrastructure.

  • Solar Panel Array
    2–12 kW capacity depending on tier. Monocrystalline preferred for high efficiency in equatorial conditions. Roof-mounted or ground frame.
  • Battery Storage
    Lithium LiFePO4 preferred. 10–50 kWh capacity for 12–24 hours autonomy. BMS with remote monitoring capability.
  • Ruggedised Container
    10–40 ft ISO shipping container, weatherproofed and ventilated. Houses all electronics. Custom branding applied by Kenostod.
  • Connectivity Hardware
    Long-range WiFi antennas (mesh capable), optional 4G/5G module for upstream, and a local compute board (Raspberry Pi 4 class or higher).
  • Compute + Storage
    NAS or SSD array per tier (500 GB to unlimited). Low-power ARM compute preferred. Kenostod provides the full software stack via OTA updates.
  • Logistics & Installation
    Partner handles shipping, customs, and on-site installation. Kenostod provides remote onboarding, node activation, and ongoing protocol support.

Revenue Streams

How the Network Generates Income

Every active Bunker creates multiple simultaneous income streams — for the operator, the hardware partner, and the community.

⛏️
Resilience Mining
Nodes earn KENO continuously for staying online. The longer the uptime streak, the higher the multiplier — powered by Euler continuous compounding.
8–25% APY
📡
Mesh Relay Fees
Every hour a Bunker relays data for a neighbouring node that's offline, it earns KENO. 60% goes to the relay node, 25% to the Foundation, 15% to Treasury.
10 KENO / hour
🛡️
Guardian Node Fees
Silver tier and above can host Guardian Node slots. Members pay KENO to have their assets protected by a Guardian — operators share the fee pool.
Per protected member
💾
Storage Economy
Community members pay KENO to store data on the Bunker's local drive. 100 KENO burned per 100 GB/month — deflationary and locally useful.
100 KENO / 100GB
